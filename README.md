## 🚀 The Three Core Services

UPMARK offers exactly **THREE** primary service divisions:

### 1. Web Development
- **Positioning**: *"Websites designed to turn your digital presence into a business asset."*
- **Description**: We design and develop modern, responsive websites that communicate your brand, showcase your offerings and make it easier for potential customers to take action.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Business website development
  - Custom website design
  - Landing page development
  - Responsive website development
  - E-commerce website development
  - Website redesign & modernisation
  - UI/UX design & design systems
  - Website performance optimisation
  - Website maintenance, where agreed

### 2. AEO Optimisation (Answer Engine Optimisation)
- **Positioning**: *"Make your business easier to discover across AI-powered search."*
- **Description**: We help businesses improve how their information is understood, discovered and referenced across AI-powered search experiences through technical optimisation, answer-focused content and stronger online business information.
- **Deliverables**:
  - AI search visibility audits
  - ChatGPT Search readiness
  - Google AI search readiness
  - Microsoft Copilot and Bing visibility readiness
  - Website crawlability and indexing review
  - Answer-focused content optimisation
  - Entity and business information optimisation
  - Relevant structured data implementation
  - FAQ and service-page optimisation
  - Competitor visibility analysis
  - AI citation and visibility monitoring
  - Monthly performance reporting
- **Market Entry Specialisation**: Initial prospecting specialisation for HVAC businesses & contractors in Texas, USA (AC repair, heat pump installation, emergency AC services, maintenance, local questions) while remaining available internationally across all suitable industries.
- **Positioning Standards**: No false ranking hacks, no guaranteed AI citations. We focus on technical crawlability, structured data, business information clarity, and answer-focused content.

### 3. Creative Agency
- **Positioning**: *"Creative that gives your brand a distinctive identity."*
- **Description**: We develop strategic visual identities and compelling creative assets that help businesses communicate their value and create a consistent brand experience.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Brand identity design
  - Logo design and brand systems
  - Brand guidelines & design tokens
  - Graphic design & marketing collateral
  - Social media creatives & post systems
  - Advertising creatives & ad variations
  - Promotional campaigns & product launch assets
  - Product creatives & packaging concepts
  - Creative direction & visual strategy
  - Short-form promotional video editing
  - Digital campaign design
